## 5. STAR Method Interview Playbook

### Scenario 1: "Describe a difficult technical bug you solved."
- **Situation:** During early field testing of NiyoSense, rural plantation ambient noise and incorrect phone distances were skewing acoustic spectrogram predictions.
- **Task:** Ensure the on-device AI only classified genuine, standardized coconut tapping events and rejected ambient background noise.
- **Action:** Implemented a multi-stage DSP defense gate: added a 1st-order 300Hz High-Pass Filter in Dart to eliminate wind and low-frequency rumble, created a standardized 3-knock cadence detection window, and calibrated softmax temperature scaling ($T=1.6$) with a 96.5% confidence cap.
- **Result:** Fused model accuracy stabilized at 92.8% in field evaluations, successfully rejecting out-of-distribution noise without requiring an active internet connection.

### Scenario 2: "Tell me about a time you had to learn a technology quickly."
- **Situation:** For Bolea Labs, I wanted to automate episodic video creation without relying on manual video editing software or heavy desktop GUI renderers.
- **Task:** Build a headless, programmatic video rendering pipeline from scratch within two weeks.
- **Action:** Researched Remotion (React-based video framework), learned the internal canvas and audio synchronization primitives, and connected it to Node.js, Gemini API for script generation, and Edge-TTS for audio synthesis.
- **Result:** Created an end-to-end factory that produced and published over 58 episodes with synchronized lip-sync and dynamic captions, which I later packaged into 6 commercial AI skills on the Agensi marketplace.
