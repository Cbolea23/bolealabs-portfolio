Bolea Labs Creator Publishing Tool

The A Day Back Then Creator Publishing Tool is a multi-platform publishing and automation software developed by Bolea Labs. Built for digital creators, history storytellers, and media operators, the tool allows users to authorize their video channels through official platform OAuth integrations (TikTok Content Posting API, YouTube Data API, Meta Graph API) to schedule, preview, and publish verified video content safely.

1

Secure OAuth Connect

Creators authenticate using their platform's official OAuth consent screen. No passwords or private keys are ever collected or stored.

2

Render & Caption Preview

Review synchronized Remotion video renders, inspect word-by-word captions, and verify claims before scheduling.

3

Human Review Gate

Every post requires deliberate one-click authorization. No automated black-box posting without explicit creator sign-off.

4

Multi-Platform Dispatch

Publishes high-definition 1080×1920 video payloads directly to TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Instagram Reels via certified REST APIs.

Compliance Notice: This software operates under official platform developer agreements. It adheres strictly to TikTok Developer Terms, the TikTok Privacy Policy, and YouTube API Services policies. For API permissions, token management, or inquiries, see our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Production Shows & Series

From world history deep-dives to Philippine dark folklore, our automated pipeline powers original episodic series across multiple formats.

A Day Back Then (Flagship)

Hour-by-hour historical reconstructions: an ancient Roman legionary's morning routine, a Viking winter voyage, Tudor kitchens, and strange true events history books left out.

Philippine Dark Lore

Tagalog-first storytelling focused on traditional Philippine mythology, folklore creatures (Aswang, Sigbin, Wakwak), haunted regional landmarks, and urban legends.

Filipino Myths (Long-Form)

Extended 8–15 minute cinematic storytelling features covering Philippine creation myths, ancient deities, pre-colonial epics, and legendary battles.

Seasonal & Horror Specials

Themed investigative series exploring historical plagues, medieval superstitions, Samhain origins, and real-life mystery archives.
