Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 27, 2026 • Operator: Bolea Labs (Christian Louis Bolea)

This Privacy Policy explains how Bolea Labs ("we", "our", "the Studio") collects, uses, and safeguards information when you browse bolealabs.site, use any Bolea Labs web application, or connect your social media or creator accounts (including TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, and Instagram) to our publishing and automation software.

1. Information We Collect

  • Website Visitors: Our portfolio and informational pages are static and do not use invasive tracking cookies, third-party analytics trackers, or require account registration for viewing.
  • Connected Creator Accounts (OAuth): When an authorized creator connects social media accounts via official platform OAuth flows (such as TikTok Login Kit, TikTok Content Posting API, Google / YouTube Data API, or Meta Graph API), we receive an OAuth access token, refresh token, and basic public identifiers (such as username, account ID, and display name) strictly as authorized by you during the consent prompt.
  • Creator Publishing Assets: Video files, captions, tags, and scheduling parameters you upload or designate for publication through our dashboard.

2. How We Use Information

We process information exclusively to provide the services you direct:

  • To upload and publish your videos, captions, and thumbnails to authorized platforms via their official developer APIs.
  • To check publication status and return post verification links and performance metrics within your operations dashboard.
  • To manage active authentication sessions securely and refresh expired tokens when authorized.
  • To reply to direct contact inquiries submitted to contact@bolealabs.site.

We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal information or media assets. We never access private direct messages, personal contacts, financial details, or unrelated account information.

3. Data Storage, Security & Token Handling

All platform API tokens and credentials are stored securely on hardened environments with encrypted storage and restricted administrative access. Tokens are never exposed in public repositories or client-facing responses. You may revoke access at any time through your connected platform's settings (for example, TikTok Security Settings → Manage App Permissions), which immediately renders stored tokens invalid.

4. Third-Party Platform Integrations

Our publishing software interacts with external platforms strictly via official APIs:

  • TikTok: Governed by TikTok Developer Terms and TikTok Privacy Policy.
  • YouTube: Governed by Google APIs Terms and Google Privacy Policy.
  • Meta (Facebook & Instagram): Governed by Meta Platform Terms and Meta Privacy Policy.

5. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any stored data associated with your account at any time. Upon receiving a written request, all associated access tokens and publishing records will be purged within 48 hours.

6. Policy Updates

We may periodically update this policy to reflect platform API compliance changes or new features. Any modifications will be posted directly on this page with an updated revision date.
